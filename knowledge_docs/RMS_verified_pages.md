## 4.4.1 End of Project (EOP) Flow stages

Source: RMS User Manual, page 44. The AI transcription cuts off after stage 3
(output was truncated), silently dropping the final stage — re-read directly
from the page image to confirm the complete flow.

1. EOP
   - One month before Project End date RMS
   - RMS auto notify Project Leader for output reporting
   - RMS auto notify for reconciliation

2. Reconcile
   - FM to prepare on final reconciliation
   - Upload approved by project leader report into RMS

3. Achievement Report
   - Project leader update project achievement
   - Verified on the finance reconciliation in the RMS
   - Email to RMC EOP required by funder

4. RMC
   - **Approved and submit to funder**

## 3.1.1 Application Flow for Internal Fund

Source: RMS User Manual, page 7.

1. Grant Opening
   - Action: RMC announce Grant opening in RMS.
   - Timeline: Submission duration is set.
   - Status: -.

2. Submission
   - Action: PL prepare and submit research grant proposal.
   - Timeline: Depend on submission; before project and appointment end date.
   - Status: Draft.

3. Faculty Recommend
   - Action: Faculty Deputy Dean or Dean review and suggest evaluation panel.
   - Timeline: 3–10 working days.
   - Status: Submit to Faculty Deputy Dean / Dean.

4. RMC
   - Action: Select panel for evaluation.
   - Timeline: 3 working days.
   - Status: RMC verification.

5. Evaluation
   - Action: Selected panel will review the proposal.
   - Timeline: 3–10 working days.
   - Status: MMU Panel Reviewer.

6. RMC Director
   - Action: RMC Director approved the proposal.
   - Timeline: 3–10 working days.
   - Status: RMC Director Approval.

## 7.2.1 Call for Quotation (External Grant)

Source: RMS User Manual, page 80.

Project Leader needs to request quotations from vendors or suppliers. Other requirements can be referred to the Purchasing Guideline for external grants. Purchase amount **more than RM100K** requires following the internal purchasing procedure. Please contact the procurement unit for further information at procurement@mmu.edu.my.

## 9.1 Postgraduate Conference Sponsorship frequency

Source: RMS User Manual, section 9.1 (near "9.1.4 Institute For Post Graduate Studies (IPS) Department").

PG Conference Sponsorship is applicable **once during each period of candidature** — since a Master's candidature and a PhD candidature are separate periods, a candidate may go **one time during the Master Programme and one time during PhD programme** (up to two times total across a Master's + PhD journey, not once overall).

This clarifies a shorter FAQ-style answer elsewhere in the manual ("eligible to apply once during the candidature") that omits the Master/PhD distinction and reads as "once, full stop" in isolation.

## 4.2.2 Ways to update budget in New Approved Project

Source: RMS User Manual, section 4.2.2 (Temporary & Contract Personnel budget update).

There are **two (2) ways** to update budget details in the New Approved Project module:
- **Add New** — add budget directly into RMS.
- **Import Budget** — upload budget details using an Excel template.

## 4.4 End of Project Checklist header fields

Source: RMS User Manual, page 46.

Before the "SECTION 1. Project Leader Verification" checklist items begin, the CHECKLIST – END OF PROJECT (EOP) (EXTERNAL GRANT) form's header has these identifying fields:
- Name (Project Leader)
- Project ID
- Grant
- REF No. (RMS)

(The Section 1 verification items themselves — Cover Letter, End of Project, Publication, Status Asset, Research Funding Record, Reconcile — are a separate list that comes AFTER this header, not the same thing as it.)
